I'm Mohamed Sahbi, a platform and developer experience engineer in Prague, Czechia. I've spent more than eight years in enterprise IT, first as a software engineer and then in DevOps, cloud automation and platform roles.

Today I work at **Atlas Copco Group** as a Cloud Automation & DevOps Engineer. I'm the product owner for **GitHub Copilot** and **Azure DevOps** across the group. I also build Azure self-service automation and Infrastructure as Code templates, and I work on the governance that keeps all of it sane at scale.

Before that I spent four years at **DHL IT Services**. I started there as a software engineer building web applications and chatbots, and later became service owner and DevOps lead for a DHL eCommerce SaaS product.

## What I care about

- **Developer experience.** Golden paths, good templates and self-service beat tickets and tribal knowledge.
- **Adoption.** A tool rollout is mostly a people problem. Governance, training, communication and a support model matter as much as the license.
- **Community.** I founded the DevOps Community of Practice at Atlas Copco, co-founded our InnerSource initiative and initiated the Software Development Lighthouse.

## Community
